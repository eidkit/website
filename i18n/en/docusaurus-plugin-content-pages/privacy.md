# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: April 1, 2026*

## Who we are

EidKit is an SDK for reading the Romanian Electronic Identity Card (CEI) via NFC. The **EidKit Demo** app demonstrates the SDK's capabilities.

## What data we collect

**We do not collect any personal data.**

The EidKit Demo app does not transmit, store, or process personal data on external servers. All data read from the identity card (name, CNP, photo, etc.) stays exclusively on your device and never leaves the app.

## Technical diagnostic data

The app uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io) for technical error reporting (crash reports). Sentry may collect:

- Device information (model, Android version)
- Call stack (stack trace) in case of a crash
- **No personal data or card data is included**

Error reports are used solely to improve app stability.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| NFC | Reading the chip on the electronic identity card |
| Storage (API 26–28) | Saving generated PDFs to the Downloads folder |
